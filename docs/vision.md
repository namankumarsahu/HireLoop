# One-year vision

ScreenScribe starts with one screening call written into one row of a shared
Airtable base.

A year later it could become the standard write-up layer for every voice
interaction in a hiring pipeline:

- every round (screen, technical, onsite) produces a structured scorecard
  automatically, not just the first screen,
- scorecards from every interviewer on a loop roll up into a single
  hiring-committee packet before the debrief,
- consistent scorecard structure across interviewers makes candidates
  comparable on the same axes, instead of five differently-shaped free-text
  notes,
- red flags and inconsistencies between what a candidate said in round 1 vs.
  round 3 get surfaced automatically instead of relying on someone remembering,
- the same pipeline extends into a real ATS (Ashby, Greenhouse, Lever) as a
  destination once a team is big enough to need one, since the hard part —
  turning a call into a structured scorecard — doesn't change with the
  destination.

The important product direction is not "more AI commentary on the
candidate." It's removing the gap between **what was actually said on the
call** and **what ends up recorded in the system everyone else relies on** —
so hiring decisions are made on what happened, not on what someone
remembered to write down.
